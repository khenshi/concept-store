# Manual QA Testing Checklist

## Purpose and scope

This is the manual QA checklist for the implemented Concept Store Management
System. It is organized by module, page, and feature and is intended for a QA
tester working against a staging or disposable environment. It covers the
browser-visible product surface defined by `docs/modules/`, including normal
journeys, negative paths, boundary conditions, access control, responsive
behavior, accessibility, security-related user checks, and recovery behavior.

This document is a test record, not a statement that the application works.
Leave every status unchecked until the exact scenario has been executed against
the candidate under test. Automated tests and historical validation notes may be
linked as supporting evidence, but they do not replace the browser checks below.

### How to record results

For each row, record the observed behavior in **Actual result**, select exactly
one status, and link a screenshot, screen recording, request ID, console log,
or test-data identifier in **Notes / evidence**. A blank status means **not
run**; it is not a pass. Mark **Blocked** only when the scenario cannot be
executed because of an environment, data, account, or dependency problem, and
describe the blocker.

Priority guidance:

- **Critical:** authentication, tenant isolation, financial correctness, data
  loss, security bypass, or a workflow that prevents the product from being
  safely used.
- **High:** core business workflow failure, incorrect authorization, broken
  primary navigation, or a severe mobile/accessibility failure.
- **Medium:** important secondary workflow or a substantial usability defect.
- **Low:** cosmetic, copy, spacing, or low-impact edge behavior.

## Test data and preconditions

Use synthetic data only. Do not reset, seed, truncate, or rewrite shared or
production financial data. Prepare enough records to exercise at least two pages
of every paginated list.

- One verified **Owner** in Organization A and a separate user in Organization B.
- One **Manager** assigned to Branch A1 but not Branch A2.
- One **Cashier** assigned to Branch A1, with no access to Branch A2.
- One linked **Merchant** profile/member and one unlinked Merchant member.
- Active and inactive branches; active and inactive, suspended, and ended
  merchant profiles; active and inactive products.
- Products with SKU, barcode, optional fields, multiple merchants, low stock,
  zero stock, and enough stock for a checkout. Include products with long names.
- Cash plus at least one enabled custom payment account requiring a reference,
  one enabled account without a reference requirement, and one disabled or
  branch-unassigned account with historical transactions.
- At least one completed sale with multiple items, mixed merchants, cash and
  non-cash payment history, and quantities that support partial and full refund.
- At least one completed refund, a refund-only report period, a zero-activity
  period, and a period containing a negative net value.
- At least two space types, multiple spaces, current occupancy, closed history,
  adjacent intervals, and an unoccupied space.
- Valid, expired, revoked, accepted, mismatched-email, and already-used
  invitation or account-action tokens.
- A test account with multiple active sessions in separate browsers or devices.
