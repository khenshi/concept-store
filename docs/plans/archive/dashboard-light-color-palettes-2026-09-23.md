# Distinct Light Dashboard Palettes

**Status:** Approved and implemented September 23, 2026.

## Additional approved scope: Distinct light dashboard palettes

Retire the six redundant violet/blue choices from the Account picker, retaining
their saved IDs as visual aliases for the nearest retained palette. Add
Sypher, Crextio, SBB, and Wellness Teal from `docs/dashboard-color-palettes.md`
as personal light themes. Keep existing preferences, account persistence, the
dark choices, and Graphite as default. Refresh the theme and account docs.

## Outcome

The picker now offers twelve themes. Sypher, Crextio, SBB, and Wellness Teal provide green, warm yellow, orange, and teal choices. Six redundant blue-violet choices were removed from the picker while their stored identifiers remain supported as Ocean or Plum visual aliases.
